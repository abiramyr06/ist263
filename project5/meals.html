Salmon Bowl

Get a delicious salmon bowl that takes 20 minutes to make and will leave you stuffed all day. One of the easiest recipes to make

Ingredients

  • Salmon
  • Rice
  • Avocado
  • Siracha Sauce
  • Mayonnaise
  • Onion Powder
  • Salt
  • Lemon Pepper Powder
  • Garlic Powder
  • Paprika

Steps

  1. If you are using canned salmon, you can skip this step. Cut your salmon into small cubes. Season with salt, lemon powder, garlic powder, paprika, and onion powder as you wish. Spray with oil and put it in an air fryer for 15 minutes at 400 degrees.
  2. Warm up your rice and cut your avocado into tiny slices as you like
  3. Get a bowl, mix the rice, salmon, and avocado with Siracha and Mayonnaise as you wish
  4. Finally, add any extra ingredients you like ad enjoy!!

Chicken and Pasta

Make a delicious, protein filled, meal that can last you 3 days. This delicious meal is both savory and healthy and most importantly, so easy to make!

Ingredients

  • Chicken Breast
  • Pasta
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Turmeric (optional)
  • Pasta Sauce
  • Garlic Powder
  • Paprika

Steps

  1. Make sure to take your chicken out earlier to allow for it to tham
  2. Cut your chicken into small pieces then put it in a Ziploc bag and season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika, and turmeric. Shake the bag to season the whole chicken.
  3. Heat your pan on the stove at medium heat and put oil. Place the chicken in the pan and wait for the color to turn to a goldish brown. Flip the chicken and do the same thing on the other side. To make sure it's properly ready, check the inside of one piece to make sure it's fully cooked
  4. In the other pot, boil water. Once the water is bubbling, place your pasta in with your desired amount. Wait for the pasta to reach appropriate consistency and drain out the water. Finally, Add your desired sauce and mix

Avocado Toast

Want an easy, delicious, and filling avocado toast you won't ever forget about. Make this easy meal that your friends will be begging for more

Ingredients

  • Sourdough Toast
  • Avocado
  • Egg
  • Everything Bagel Seasoning
  • Chil Flakes
  • Garlic Powder
  • Onion Powder

Steps

  1. Toast your bread
  2. Get a pan and put a little bit of oil and place on medium heatCrack an egg and wait for the white part to solidify and leave the yolk not fully cooked (it should be able to jiggle a little). Should take roughly 5 minutes to cook
  3. Cut your avocado, smash it, and spread it on your toast
  4. Season with everythng bagel seasoning, garlic powder, onion powder, and chili flakes
  5. Finally, place your egg on top and enjoy
