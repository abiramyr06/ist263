Snack Ideas

Air Fried Potatoes

Do you want easy, filling tiny snacks to munch on that are healthy and easy to make in bulk? Make these deliciously easy potato bite!

Ingredients

  • Potatos
  • Onion Powder
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Garlic Powder

Steps

  1. Cut your Potaoes into tiny cubes
  2. Season with garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper
  3. Spray with oil and place in air fryer. Leave to cook for 7-15 minutes depending on how crispy you want them.

Fish Patties

Make this recipe if you want delicious and healthy snacks that you can take to snack on in between classes!

Ingredients

  • Potatos
  • onion
  • green pepper
  • curry leaf
  • Mustard Seed
  • Fennel Seed
  • Sardines
  • Fennel Powder
  • Fenugreek Powder
  • Patties
  • Egg Yolk
  • Onion Powder
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Garlic Powder

Steps

  1. Boil 8 potatoes and then peel and mush them. Season with a little salt
  2. Cut up a full onion, small green pepper, and about 3 rows of curry leaves if you want
  3. Put sardine oil in a pot and add mustard seed, fennel seed, and the ingredients above. Let it cook for a little and then add sardines in mushedSeason with pepper, fennel powder, and fenugreek powder
  4. Mix it with the potatoes, add salt, and take it off the stove. Wait for it to cool down.
  5. Get premade patties and start filling it with the mix. Place the ingredients in the middle of the patty and close it by pushing the ends together. (Line the edges with water to close the patties). Cover the patty in egg yolk
  6. Place in deep fryer at maximum heat. Wait one minute and flip. Repeat until all patties are fried

Chocolate Chip Cookies

Do you want mouthwatering chocolate cookies that you can make at home with your friend. Try this recipe for the best chocolate chip cookies!

Ingredients

  • Chocolate
  • Baking Soda
  • All Purpose Flour
  • curry leaf
  • Melted Butter
  • Brown Sugar
  • Granulated Sugar
  • Vanilla Extract
  • Egg

Steps

  1. Melt 9 tbsp or less of chocolate then make circles of them and place in fridge for at least 30 minutes and preheat oven to 350 F
  2. In the first bowl, mix 1/2 tsp of baking soda and 1 1/4 cup of all-purpose flour
  3. In another bowl whisk 1/2 cup of melted butter, 1/2 cup of brown sugar, and 1/3 cup of granulated sugar.
  4. In the second bowl crack an egg and add 1 tbsp of vanilla extract and whisk.
  5. Combine the bowl with dry ingredients to the second bowl and add the chocolate chips made earlier after your mix
  6. Place on tray and bake for roughly 12-15 minutes, check it to see when they are at a consistency of your liking.
  7. Leave out for 30 minutes to get a softer cookie. And then Enjoy!!
